name: Owner account retention policy
description: Owner-requested deletion is a retained-data account status, not destructive cascade or personal-data erasure.
---

For garage-owner account deletion, the user said: “instead of deletion make status as inactive or deleted. just add one status because in the future we need to get that data as well”.

**Why:** The user wants the data available for future retrieval rather than permanently deleting the account's garage and linked records.

**How to apply:** Preserve owner and linked business records when owners request account deletion; deny access and distinguish deleted from ordinarily inactive accounts. Do not silently change this into physical deletion or anonymization. Explain that retaining personal data and only deactivating/archiving may not satisfy Apple's deletion requirement; do not guarantee App Store approval.
